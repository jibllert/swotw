The Great Pyrimid of Giza

The Great Pyrimid of Giza is the largest of the Egyption pyrimids and the most famous landmark of the Giza pyrimid complex in Giza, Egypt. It is the oldest of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World, and the only wonder that has remained largly intact. The Great Pyramid served as the tomb of Egyptian Pharaoh Khufu who ruled during the Fourth Dynasty of the Old Kingdom. It was built 2600 BC over a period of about 26 years

  • Initialy standing 146.6 metres tall
  • 146.6 metres tall
  • 6 million tonnes in total
  • Located in Giza,Egypt
  • Built ~4600 years ago
